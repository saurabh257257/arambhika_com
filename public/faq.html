Ordering, sizes and delivery

What is the difference between nickel-plated strip and pure nickel strip?

Nickel-plated strip is steel coated with a layer of nickel, offering good conductivity and corrosion resistance at a lower cost. Pure nickel strip is solid nickel, offering lower resistance, better conductivity and superior corrosion resistance — ideal for high-performance battery packs and EV applications. Nickel-Plated Strip, Explained

Can I use nickel-plated strip for a 48 V e-bike battery?

Often yes, if the current per strip stays within a safe limit. For heavy-current packs use a thicker strip, more strips in parallel, or pure nickel. Share your pack current with us and we will suggest a size.

What does 2P and CD mean in the product names?

2P means one strip joins two cells in parallel. CD is the centre-to-centre distance between those two cells. Pick the CD that matches your cell holder or pack layout.

How should I store nickel strip during the monsoon?

Keep it sealed in a dry bag with silica gel and avoid opening coils in humid air. Dry strip welds more consistently.

Pure Nickel Strip for High-Current Packs

Is pure nickel strip worth the extra cost?

For high-current, hot-climate or long-life packs, usually yes. The lower resistance cuts heat at the joints and the strip stays clean. For small low-current packs, plated strip is often enough.

Which pure nickel strip fits 21700 cells?

Use a 2P strip made for the 21700 format, or a 1P strip in the width your layout needs. The listings show width, thickness and centre distance so you can match your holder.

What are zig-zag nickel strips used for?

Zig-zag strips flex slightly, which helps absorb cell movement and thermal expansion. We list zig-zag options for 18650 and 33140 cells.

Can I weld pure nickel with my existing spot welder?

Most pack makers do. Pure nickel is softer than plated steel, so start with lower energy and test pull strength on a sample before production.

Tin-Coated Copper Busbars

What size copper busbar do I need for a 100 Ah LFP cell pack?

It depends on your continuous current, not the cell capacity alone. As a rough start, a 20 x 2 mm bar (40 mm²) suits tens of amps; for higher current use a larger section or parallel bars. Send us your pack current and we will advise.

Why is the busbar tin coated?

Tin protects the copper from tarnishing, which keeps joint resistance low and stable, especially in humid and coastal Indian conditions.

Do you make custom length copper busbars?

Is copper better than aluminium for battery busbars?

Copper conducts better, so a copper bar of the same section runs cooler. Aluminium is lighter and cheaper but needs a larger section and careful joint treatment.

Spot-Welding Nickel Strip, Step by Step

What energy setting should I use for 0.15 mm nickel strip?

It depends on your welder, so start at the lowest setting and raise it step by step on a scrap cell. Stop when the pull test tears the strip, not the weld.

Why do my welds peel off the cell?

The usual causes are too little energy, a dirty or oxidised surface, or low electrode pressure. Clean the strip and raise the energy a little.

How many weld spots do I need per cell terminal?

Most builders use at least two per terminal. High-current packs often use more or a wider strip. Check with your design and test pull strength.

Can I spot-weld 0.30 mm strip with a hobby welder?

Many hobby welders struggle with 0.30 mm. Test on scrap first, and consider thinner strip in parallel layers if your machine cannot weld it reliably.

Does pure nickel need different settings from plated strip?

Often a little lower energy, because pure nickel is softer. Test each strip type and thickness on your machine.

Build a 48V Scooter Battery Pack

How many cells do I need for a 48V pack?

A 13S Li-ion pack needs 13 cells in series for about 48 V. Multiply by the parallel count for capacity. For example, 13S6P needs 78 cells.

What strip joins the cells in a parallel group?

Most builders use a 2P pre-cut strip or a plain strip across the group. Pick a width that matches your cell spacing and weld pattern.

Why do series links need thicker strip?

Series links carry the full pack current, while each parallel cell carries only a share of it.

Is 48 V LFP different from 48 V Li-ion?

Yes. LFP packs usually use 16 cells in series (about 51.2 V), against 13 for Li-ion. Chargers, BMS and cut-off voltages are different.

Can I use plated strip for an e-scooter?

For low-current builds, often yes. For heavy-current or hot-climate scooters we recommend pure nickel or parallel strips. Send us your pack current and we will suggest a size.

LFP Packs for Solar and Inverters

Can I replace my 12V lead-acid inverter battery with a 4S LFP pack?

Often yes, if the inverter charger has an LFP profile or a compatible setting and the BMS supports your load. Check your inverter manual and keep the BMS rated above peak current.

Which strip fits 32650 LFP cells?

A 2P strip made for the 32650 format, with or without holder, depending on your layout. See the listings for width, thickness and centre distance.

Do prismatic LFP cells use nickel strip?

Usually no. Large prismatic cells use bolted terminals and flat copper busbars.

What size busbar for a 100 Ah LFP cell?

It depends on your continuous current. A 20 x 2 mm bar suits tens of amps; heavier loads need a larger section or parallel bars.

Is LFP safer than regular lithium-ion?

LFP is generally more thermally stable, but pack design, BMS and installation still matter.

Size Your Nickel Strip Right

How do I find the current rating of nickel strip?

There is no single rating, because it depends on allowed temperature rise, strip length and cooling. Calculate resistance and heat for your current as shown here, then confirm with a load test.

What is the resistance of 8 x 0.15 mm nickel strip?

For pure nickel, about 0.58 mΩ per 10 mm of length, from 0.07 Ω·mm²/m divided by the 1.2 mm² cross-section.

Is plated steel strip worse than pure nickel for current?

Yes. The steel core has higher resistance, so the same size runs hotter. It remains fine for lighter loads.

How can I carry more current without changing the strip size?

Add strips in parallel, shorten the path, or move the main path to copper busbar.

Do 2P strips carry more current than 1P?

A wider 2P strip has more cross-section, but its cut-outs reduce it. Measure the narrowest section when you calculate.

Why Your Battery Pack Runs Hot

How hot is too hot for a lithium battery pack?

Follow your cell maker’s data sheet. If the case or any strip is too hot to hold, or one spot is much hotter than the rest, investigate before using the pack further.

How do I know if a spot weld is weak?

Do a pull test on a sample, or measure the voltage drop across the weld under load. A high reading compared with neighbouring welds suggests a weak joint.

Can loose busbar bolts cause heating?

Yes. A loose joint has higher resistance and heats up. Clean the faces and retighten to the cell maker’s torque.

Will pure nickel fix a hot pack?

It lowers strip resistance, which helps. But weld quality and joint design also matter, so test after changing.

Why do packs heat more in Indian summer?

High ambient temperature leaves less margin, and closed enclosures trap heat. Design for the hottest conditions your product will meet.

Nickel Strip Price per kg

Why is pure nickel strip so much costlier than plated?

Pure nickel is nickel all the way through, while plated strip is mostly steel with a thin nickel layer. Nickel is much more expensive than steel.

Does the price per kg change with thickness?

Often yes. Thinner or narrower strip needs more processing per kg, and special forms such as 2P or zig-zag add manufacturing cost.

How many metres are in 1 kg of 8 x 0.15 mm strip?

Roughly 93 m of pure nickel or about 106 m of plated steel strip, from material density and the strip cross-section.

Packs for E-Rickshaws and E-Autos

Is copper busbar or nickel strip better for e-rickshaw packs?

It depends on the cell format. Large prismatic LFP cells use bolted copper busbar; cylindrical-cell packs use spot-welded nickel strip. Most new e-rickshaw packs in India use prismatic cells.

How often should busbar bolts be checked on a swapping fleet?

There is no universal interval — follow your pack maker’s maintenance schedule. As a general practice, include torque checks in periodic swap-station servicing, since repeated handling and vibration can loosen joints over time.

Can plated nickel strip handle e-rickshaw current loads?

Often yes for standard loads, but e-rickshaws draw more current than a typical two-wheeler. Size the strip for your actual continuous and peak current, and consider pure nickel for higher-load or long-life fleet packs.

Power-Tool Battery Packs

Should I use plated or pure nickel strip for a power tool battery?

Pure nickel is the standard choice for power tools because of the high peak current during use. Plated strip is better suited to lower-current, cost-sensitive packs.

Does vibration affect nickel strip welds?

Yes. Repeated vibration can fatigue a weld over time. Tools and drones benefit from extra weld points and strip layouts that avoid long unsupported spans.

What strip size suits a drone battery pack?

It depends on your motor’s peak current draw and your weight budget. Calculate the cross-section needed for your peak current, then weigh that against how much strip weight your drone can carry.

UPS and Telecom Backup Packs

Why use tin-coated copper busbar for telecom backup packs?

Tin coating resists tarnishing better than bare copper, which helps keep joint resistance low and stable over years of standby duty, especially in humid or coastal sites.

How is sizing different for a UPS pack versus an EV pack?

Size for your worst-case discharge current during an outage and how long outages typically last in your area, rather than for frequent high-current cycling like an EV sees.

Is pure nickel strip necessary for a small home UPS pack?

For smaller, lower-current home UPS packs, plated strip may be adequate. For telecom towers or data centre backup with higher sustained currents, pure nickel is the safer choice.

Solar Street-Light Packs

Is plated nickel strip good enough for solar street lights?

For most solar street light and lantern packs, yes, since current draw is low. The priority is outdoor durability and consistent quality across production, not current capacity.

How many years should a solar light battery pack last?

This depends on your cell choice, usage pattern and build quality, and is a separate question from interconnect choice. Confirm expected life with your cell supplier and test under your actual outdoor conditions.

Should I use pure nickel for export-market solar lights?

Consider it for harsh environments (coastal, desert, high-humidity) or where your product carries a longer warranty, since it offers more corrosion margin. For standard domestic programmes, plated strip is usually sufficient.

Choosing a Strip and Busbar Supplier

How many sample batches should I test before switching suppliers?

There is no fixed number, but testing at least two to three batches over a few weeks helps reveal batch-to-batch consistency, which a single sample cannot show.

What should I check on a cancelled cheque or GST invoice from a supplier?

Confirm the GSTIN matches the legal entity you are dealing with, and that the HSN code on the invoice matches the product category, for your own input tax credit and compliance records.

Is a lower price always a red flag for nickel strip?

Not necessarily, but an unusually low price versus other quotes is worth investigating specifically: ask about the grade, plating thickness, or copper purity to understand what is actually different.

Bulk Nickel Strip: Mistakes to Avoid

How much tolerance is normal on nickel strip thickness?

This varies by manufacturer and grade. Ask your supplier for the specific tolerance on your size, and if your welding process is tolerance-sensitive, request a sample from the actual production batch.

How long should I store nickel strip before it degrades?

Properly sealed with silica gel in dry conditions, strip can be stored for extended periods. The main risk is humidity exposure, particularly during the monsoon, which causes surface oxidation.

Should I always order the cheapest quote?

Not without checking what is behind the price: grade, plating thickness, tolerance and lead time all affect whether a cheaper quote is actually a better deal for your specific pack design.

Smart BMS vs Hardware BMS

Is a smart BMS safer than a hardware BMS?

Both protect against the same basic faults. A smart BMS adds visibility and adjustable settings, which helps you catch problems early, but safe protection depends on the BMS being correctly rated and wired.

Can I use a smart BMS without the phone app?

Yes. It protects the pack like any BMS. The app is optional and used for monitoring and settings.

Does a smart BMS talk to every inverter?

No. Inverter communication depends on the protocol the inverter supports and the BMS model. Check the inverter manual and the BMS specification before ordering.

LFP vs NMC BMS: Pick the Series Count

How many cells in series for a 48V LFP pack?

Most 48 V LFP packs use 15S (about 48 V nominal) or 16S (about 51.2 V nominal). Check what your charger and controller are designed for.

Can I use a 16S BMS on a 15S pack?

No. The BMS monitors each series group, so the cell count has to match the board.

Is 13S NMC the same as 48V?

A 13S NMC pack is about 46.8 V nominal and is sold as a 48 V pack. Fully charged it sits near 54.6 V.

Active Balancing BMS for Home Solar

Do I need active balancing for a small battery?

Not usually. For small packs with few cells, normal balancing is often enough. Active balancing pays off on large cells and long-life storage.

Is 15S or 16S better for solar storage?

Both are used. 16S gives about 51.2 V and is widely used with 48 V class inverters. 15S gives about 48 V. Follow your inverter manufacturer's recommended range.

Will the BMS balance during discharge?

That depends on the model and its settings. Check the specification of the exact board you choose.

Choosing a BMS Current Rating

How many amps BMS do I need for 1000W?

At 48 V, 1000 W is about 21 A, so a 40 A to 60 A board gives margin. At 12 V the same load is about 83 A, which needs a 100 A class board or more.

Is the peak or continuous rating more important?

Both. Choose the continuous rating for your normal load and check that the BMS peak rating covers motor start-up or inverter surge.

Can two BMS be used in parallel?

Do not parallel BMS boards on one pack unless the manufacturer says it is supported. Use a single higher-rated board instead.

Cell Holders for Battery Packs

What does 1X2 mean on a cell holder?

It describes the layout: one row of two cells. Join holders together to form larger blocks.

Do I need holders if I am spot-welding strip?

Not strictly, but they keep spacing even, make welding easier and give a more rigid pack. Most professional packs use them.

Can a holder replace glue or tape?

Holders position the cells but you still need the pack case or wrap to keep it together. Follow your pack design.

Potting Compound for Battery Packs

What does 1:1 mean on potting compound?

It means the two parts are mixed in equal amounts. Check the datasheet to see whether that is by weight or by volume.

Can I remove potted electronics later?

Silicone gel is easier to cut away than rigid epoxy, but repair is still difficult. Plan accordingly.

Is potting required by law?

Requirements depend on your product and its standards. Check them for your pack type before deciding.

Aluminium Busbar Guide

What does C2C mean?

It stands for cell to cell: the busbar is designed to connect one cell to another.

Can I weld aluminium busbar to the cell?

Some prismatic cells are laser welded, others are bolted. Follow the cell maker for the method it supports.

Do I need anti-oxidation paste?

Many builders use a contact compound on aluminium joints. Follow the cell and busbar maker's guidance for your build.

Copper vs Aluminium Busbar

Is copper busbar always better?

It carries more current for the same size and resists joint problems well, but it costs more and weighs more. The best choice depends on your design.

Why is copper tin coated?

The coating protects the copper from oxidising and gives a stable surface for bolted joints.

Can I mix copper and aluminium in one pack?

It is possible but dissimilar metals can corrode at the joint in damp air. Avoid direct contact unless the design handles it.

Build a 51.2V 314Ah Storage Battery

How many kWh is a 16S 314Ah pack?

About 16 kWh nominal (51.2 V x 314 Ah). Usable energy depends on the depth of discharge you allow.

Do I need to compress prismatic cells?

Cell makers usually specify a compression range to keep cells flat over life. Follow the datasheet for the exact cell you buy.

Can I build a 24V or 12V pack from 314Ah cells?

Yes, with 8S (25.6 V) or 4S (12.8 V). Current rises as voltage falls, so check the BMS and busbar rating.

100Ah and 150Ah LFP Prismatic Cells

Can 100Ah cells be paralleled?

Yes, for more capacity. Match the cells, connect them with equal-length busbar, and use a BMS rated for the total current.

Are LFP cells a drop-in for a lead-acid battery?

A 4S LFP pack is about 12.8 V, which suits many 12 V systems, but the charger and any alternator must support lithium charging.

Build a 12V 100Ah LFP Battery

Is a 12V 100Ah LFP battery equal to a 100Ah lead-acid battery?

It holds more usable energy, because LFP can be discharged much deeper than lead-acid. Compare usable watt-hours rather than amp-hours alone.

Do I need a special charger?

Use a charger with an LFP profile, or one whose settings you can set to the right voltages for four LFP cells in series.

Is it safe to build my own?

It needs care: clean joints, correct wiring order, proper fusing and a rated BMS. If unsure, use a qualified pack builder.

LFP 32140 Cylindrical Cells

What does 32140 mean?

The cell is about 32 mm in diameter and 140 mm long. Check the datasheet for the exact dimensions.

Is 32140 the same as 32650?

No. 32650 is about 65 mm long. Both are 32 mm wide, which is why some strip and holders suit both. Check fit before ordering.

Can I use pure nickel strip with LFP cells?

Yes. Pure nickel carries more current for the same size, which helps on higher-rate packs.

18650 vs 21700 NMC Cells

Is a 21700 always better than an 18650?

It holds more energy per cell, but the best choice depends on space, current needs and your tooling.

What does EV grade mean?

It indicates a cell offered for higher-rate vehicle use. Always read the datasheet for the actual continuous and peak current rating.

Can I mix 18650 and 21700 in one pack?

No. Different sizes need different holders and strip, and different cell types should not be mixed in a series string.

Build a 13S 48V 21700 Pack

What does 13S4P mean?

13 cell groups in series, with 4 cells in parallel in each group. That is 52 cells in total.

How far can this pack take a scooter?

It depends on the vehicle, load and riding. As a rough guide, 842 Wh at 25 Wh per km would be about 33 km. Treat it as an estimate only.

Should I use plated or pure strip?

Plated is the economical choice for moderate current. Pure nickel carries more current for the same size, suiting higher loads.

Nickel Strip Centre Distance (CD)

What is the difference between 1P and 2P strip?

1P strip is a plain strip used to join cells in whatever layout you need. 2P strip is pre-shaped to join two cells in parallel and link to the next group.

What does CD stand for?

Centre distance, the spacing between the centres of two adjacent cells in the layout.

Can I use CD 23 strip in a CD 22.5 holder?

Not without checking. A half-millimetre difference can move the weld point off the terminal. Match the CD to your holder.

Slit, Fuse and Zig-Zag Nickel Strip

Does a fuse-type strip replace a BMS?

No. It adds cell-level protection in certain faults, but the BMS and main fuse are still required.

Are slit strips easier to weld?

It depends on your welder and settings. Run test welds on both before committing to a batch.

Nickel Strip Thickness: 0.15, 0.20, 0.30

Is 0.30 mm strip always better?

It carries more current, but it needs a stronger welder and gives more heat at the joint. Use the thickness your current and welder require.

Can I stack two thin strips instead of one thick one?

Some builders do. Test the weld quality, since stacked strip can be harder to weld reliably.

Which is better for e-bikes?

It depends on the motor current and the cells. Many e-bike packs use 0.15 to 0.20 mm strip; high-power builds may need more.

Battery Pack Bill of Materials

Do I need a spot welder for every pack?

Only for strip-welded cylindrical packs. Prismatic cells use bolted or laser-welded busbar, so no spot welder is needed.

What is the part people forget most?

The main fuse and insulation. A BMS alone is not a replacement for a correctly rated fuse and clean insulation.

Can I buy everything from one place?

Yes. Cells, strip, busbar, holders, BMS, epoxy sheet and potting compound are all on our store.

Buying LFP Cells: A Checklist

What does A-grade mean?

It is the supplier's grading term and is not a single universal standard. Ask for the datasheet and the test data behind the grade.

Should I test every cell?

For expensive or critical packs, yes. At minimum, measure the voltage and internal resistance of every cell and capacity-test a sample.

Can I mix cells bought at different times?

Avoid it. Different batches and ages age differently, which causes balancing problems. Build the whole pack from one batch.
