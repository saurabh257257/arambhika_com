Nickel Strip

Nickel Strip for 32650 Cells

Widths 46.5 to 49.5 mm, 0.15 to 0.30 mm thick

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

32650 and 32140 LFP cells are wide, so their strip is wide too. This range has the most variants of any cell size.

Reading the range

  • Width 46.5 mm: plated 0.15, plated 0.20, pure 0.15
  • Width 47 mm: plated 0.30, pure 0.20, pure 0.30
  • Width 49.5 mm: pure 0.20

CD 32.8 vs 34.5

32.8 suits cells packed tight without a holder; 34.5 suits cells spaced by a holder. Measure your layout first. See the CD guide.

Choosing thickness

0.15 mm for light loads, 0.20 mm for most storage packs, 0.30 mm for high current if your welder can weld it. Use the thickness guide and the sizing guide.

Your cells

These strips connect 32140 and 32650 LFP cells. Use 32650 holders where the layout calls for them.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Why so many variants?

Different cell makers, holders and current needs call for different widths, spacing and thickness.

Which is best for a 15Ah 32140 pack?

Match the spacing and use the thickness your current and welder allow; message us your layout.

Is 0.30 mm hard to weld?

It needs a stronger welder than 0.15 mm. Test before ordering a large quantity.
