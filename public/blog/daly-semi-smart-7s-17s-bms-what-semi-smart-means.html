BMS Guide

Daly 7S-17S Semi-Smart BMS

One adjustable board for many pack sizes

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

A semi-smart BMS sits between a fixed hardware board and a fully smart one. The Daly 7S-17S 40A board is built to serve many pack sizes with a single part number.

What "semi-smart" gives you

It offers some of the settings and monitoring of a smart BMS without the full feature set. Details vary by model, so check the specification of the Daly 7S-17S 40A semi-smart BMS for exactly what it reads and lets you change.

Why a wide series range helps

A workshop building packs of different sizes can stock one board instead of many. The same unit might serve a 10S, 13S or 16S build once set up for the cell count. That reduces inventory and mistakes.

Who it suits

  • Pack assemblers with varied orders.
  • Repair shops matching mixed pack sizes.
  • Builders who want some monitoring without paying for the top tier.

Alternatives

For 100 A or Bluetooth with RS485 and CAN, see the smart 7S-24S range. If the pack size never changes, a fixed board is simpler.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

How do I tell the board my cell count?

Follow the setup steps in the manual for the model. Some boards detect or are configured through an app.

Is a semi-smart BMS safe for LFP and NMC?

Check that the chemistry is supported and set correctly before charging. Wrong settings can overcharge cells.

Can it replace my hardware BMS?

If the series count and current fit, usually yes. Confirm the wiring layout first.
