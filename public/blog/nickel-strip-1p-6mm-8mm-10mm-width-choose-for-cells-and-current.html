1P Nickel Strip: 6, 8 or 10 mm?

Width, thickness and current in one table

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Plain 1P strip is the most flexible option. Three widths and several thicknesses cover most hand-built packs.

Cross-section

Cross-section is width times thickness. It decides how much current the strip carries.

Width and the cell

A 18650 terminal can fit a 8 mm or 10 mm strip with room for two weld spots side by side. 6 mm is tight and best for single spots or small cells. Wider is not always better if it covers the vent area of the cell.

Rules of thumb

  • Choose the strip so that each cell's current passes through enough cross-section.
  • Parallel groups carry the sum of their cells' current, so strip between groups needs more.
  • Use pure nickel to get more current from a given size.

For worked examples see the sizing guide, and for plated vs pure the price guide.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Which width is best for 18650?

Many builders use 8 mm or 10 mm. Pick by the current and the weld spot layout.

Can I use 1P strip for 21700?

Yes. The same widths fit, and thicker strip may be needed for more current.

Is thicker or wider better?

Both raise cross-section. Thicker needs a stronger welder; wider needs room on the terminal.
