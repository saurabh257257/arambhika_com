SB50 Connector Sets

Choose the set, crimp it, wire it safely

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

An SB50 is a compact two-pole plug in the 50-amp class, used on batteries, chargers and inverter leads. It lets you disconnect a pack without cutting wires.

What is in a set

A set holds the plastic housings and the metal contacts. The contacts here are crimped on the wire end. We list sets with two different thimbles, so choose by the wire you will use.

Choosing the set

  1. Check the wire size. The thimble has a range of wire sizes it accepts. Open the product page for the set, or ask us, and match it to your wire. Our silicone wire guide lists the common sizes.
  2. Check the current. The connector is a 50-amp class part. Your wire, fuse and BMS should be sized so the current stays below the connector rating, with margin.
  3. Think about both ends. Charger and battery leads that plug together must use compatible connectors.

Crimping

  • Strip only the length the contact needs, and keep all wire strands in the barrel.
  • Use a crimp tool made for this type of contact. Pliers crush the barrel unevenly and give a poor joint.
  • Tug the wire hard after crimping. If it moves, cut it off and redo it.
  • Fit the contact into the housing until it clicks, and check it cannot be pulled out.

Polarity and safety

  • Keep a fixed convention across all your packs and chargers, for example red for positive and black for negative, and check it with a meter before connecting.
  • Put a fuse close to the battery, on the positive lead.
  • Never leave a live pack with an exposed contact. Cover unused connectors with their cap or tape.

Sets in our range: SB50 with 6 thimble and SB50 with 10/12 thimble. Wire to match: 10 AWG red and black.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What is a thimble?

The metal contact crimped onto the wire. Different thimbles suit different wire sizes.

Can I solder instead of crimping?

Use the crimp method the connector is designed for. If you solder, do it as the maker recommends and never leave a cold joint.

How do I know which set fits my wire?

Check the wire range on the product page, or send us your wire size on WhatsApp.
