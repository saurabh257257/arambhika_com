Prismatic LFP Cells by kWh

Energy per cell and how to compare cost

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

A cell is easier to compare once you turn amp-hours into kilowatt-hours. It takes one multiplication.

Energy per cell

Energy in kWh is nominal voltage times capacity divided by 1000. An LFP cell is 3.2 V nominal.

CapacityEnergy per cell16S pack (51.2 V)
100 Ah0.32 kWh5.1 kWh
150 Ah0.48 kWh7.7 kWh
206 Ah0.66 kWh10.5 kWh
230 Ah0.74 kWh11.8 kWh
280 Ah0.90 kWh14.3 kWh
314 Ah1.00 kWh16.1 kWh

A 16S pack uses 16 cells in series for 51.2 V nominal. One cell string of 314Ah cells gives about 16 kWh, which is a common size for home storage.

Compare cost per kWh

Divide the cell price by its energy in kWh. For example, if a 314Ah cell is priced at P rupees, the cost per kWh is P divided by 1.005. Use the live price on the product page, since prices change. Work it out for two or three capacities before you decide: a larger cell often costs less per kWh, but you need fewer of them, so check the minimum order as well.

Fewer big cells or more small cells

  • Big cells mean fewer joints, a simpler pack and fewer cells to balance.
  • Small cells are easier to handle and let you size the pack in smaller steps.
  • Each cell needs a busbar joint, so a 16S pack of 314Ah cells has far fewer connections than a 16S4P pack of 100Ah cells.

Examples from our range: 100Ah, 206Ah, 280Ah and 314Ah. For a build with step-by-step wiring see Build a 51.2V 314Ah Storage Battery.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn Ah into kWh?

Multiply nominal voltage by amp-hours and divide by 1000. For an LFP cell that is 3.2 x Ah / 1000.

Is a bigger cell always better value?

Often it costs less per kWh, but check the minimum order and the size of the pack you need.

Can I mix different capacities in one pack?

It is not recommended. Cells in the same pack should have the same capacity and come from the same batch.
