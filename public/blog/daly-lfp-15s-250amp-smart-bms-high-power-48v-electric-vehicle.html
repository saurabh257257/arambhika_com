BMS Guide

Daly 15S 250A BMS for 48V EVs

For motors that outgrow a 100A board

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Most 48 V vehicles are happy with a 60 A to 100 A BMS. A few go well past that, and for those the 250A board is the right tool.

What 250A means in power

At about 48 V, 250 A is around 12 kW of peak power. That is the territory of strong scooters, three-wheel cargo vehicles and small utility EVs. For typical e-scooters, a smaller board such as the 15S 100A or 15S 80A is enough.

Check three things first

  1. Controller current. Look at the controller's battery current limit, not the motor's peak.
  2. Cell rating. Prismatic LFP cells of 100Ah rated 1C give 100 A; you need cells or parallel strings that support the load.
  3. Interconnects. Use copper busbar, not thin nickel strip, at this current.

The 15S 250A smart BMS

It brings Bluetooth monitoring to a high-current pack, so you can watch current and cell voltages during a hard ride or load test. Mount it with good airflow and keep the main cables short and thick.

Do not oversize blindly

A 250A board on a 40A system is wasted money. Size to your measured peak with margin, using our rating guide.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Which controller needs a 250A BMS?

Controllers whose battery current limit approaches that figure. Read the limit in the controller specification.

Is 48V LFP 15S or 16S?

Both are used. Match the BMS to the series count of your cells and to what the charger and controller expect.

Can nickel strip carry 250A?

Not safely in a normal pack. Use copper busbar or multiple parallel paths designed for the current.
