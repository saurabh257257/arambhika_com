What "inverter version" means

The 8S 100A inverter board is listed for inverter-type loads. Ask us if your inverter has a pre-charge or surge need, since inverter inputs can draw a large initial current.

When a hardware board is the right call

  • The pack is cheap, sealed or rarely serviced.
  • You do not need data or settings.
  • You want to keep the cost down.

If you do want data, see the JBD smart range. Compare with Daly hardware boards.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Can a hardware BMS be reconfigured?

Thresholds are fixed. Choose the board for your series count and chemistry up front.

Which board for a 48V e-scooter?

A 15S LFP board with a current rating above your controller limit, such as the 60A or 100A.

Do these balance cells?

Most provide basic balancing. Check the specification of your board.
