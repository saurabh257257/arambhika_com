Nickel Strip Centre Distance (CD)

Match 2P strip to 18650, 21700 and 32650

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Product names like "2P(21700) 31x0.15 mm CD 22.5 with holder" pack a lot of information into a few characters. Here is how to read them.

Decoding the name

PartMeaning
1P or 2PStrip made to join one cell, or two cells side by side (parallel)
(21700)The cell size it is made for
31x0.15 mmWidth x thickness of the strip
CD 22.5Centre distance: the spacing between cell centres, in mm
with / without holderWhether it suits a plastic cell holder layout
with / without slitWhether the strip has a slit cut in it

Why CD matters

If the strip's centre distance does not match your cell spacing, the weld points will not land on the terminals. CD is a little more than the cell diameter. For example, 18650 strip uses CD 19, 21700 uses CD 22.5 or 23, and 32650 uses CD 32.8 or 34.5, depending on the holder or gap you are using.

Choosing by cell

  • 18650: plated 26x0.15 CD 19 or pure 26x0.15
  • 21700: plated CD 23 with holder or pure CD 23
  • 32650: plated CD 34.5 with holder or pure CD 34.5

When in doubt

Measure the centre-to-centre spacing in your holder and send us a photo of your layout. Read the strip type guide to choose a design, and the cell holder guide for spacing.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 1P and 2P strip?

1P strip is a plain strip used to join cells in whatever layout you need. 2P strip is pre-shaped to join two cells in parallel and link to the next group.

What does CD stand for?

Centre distance, the spacing between the centres of two adjacent cells in the layout.

Can I use CD 23 strip in a CD 22.5 holder?

Not without checking. A half-millimetre difference can move the weld point off the terminal. Match the CD to your holder.
