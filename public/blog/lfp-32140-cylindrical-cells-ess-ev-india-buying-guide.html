LFP Cells

LFP 32140 Cylindrical Cells

A 15Ah cell for compact ESS and EV packs

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

The 32140 cell is a round LFP cell, 32 mm across and 140 mm long, rated 3.2 V and 15 Ah. It lets pack makers build LFP packs with the same spot-welded methods used for 18650 cells.

Why use cylindrical LFP

  • Modular: build any voltage and capacity by series and parallel groups.
  • Compact and sturdy, suited to portable and vehicle packs.
  • Spot-welded with strip, so no bolted terminals to loosen.

Reading the grade

The listings name the intended use. ESS A-grade cells suit storage, with steady cycling. EV cells are meant for higher-rate use. Our range includes JSK ESS A-grade, CNAE ESS A-grade, Mocean and CBAK EV. Match the grade to your duty and check the datasheet's current rating.

Connecting the cells

These cells are joined with nickel strip made for the 32650 and 32140 size. Pure and plated options with holders are available, such as the 32650 plated 2P strip. Size the strip to the current as shown in our strip sizing guide. Check the strip's centre distance against your cell spacing; the CD guide explains how.

Example pack

A 16S pack gives about 51.2 V. With 4 cells in parallel per group (16S4P, 64 cells), capacity is 60 Ah, about 3 kWh nominal. Pair it with a 16S BMS, and see the LFP solar pack guide.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What does 32140 mean?

The cell is about 32 mm in diameter and 140 mm long. Check the datasheet for the exact dimensions.

Is 32140 the same as 32650?

No. 32650 is about 65 mm long. Both are 32 mm wide, which is why some strip and holders suit both. Check fit before ordering.

Can I use pure nickel strip with LFP cells?

Yes. Pure nickel carries more current for the same size, which helps on higher-rate packs.
