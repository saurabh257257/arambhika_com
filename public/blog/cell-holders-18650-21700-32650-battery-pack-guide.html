Cell Holders for Battery Packs

18650, 21700, 32650 and larger sizes

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

A cell holder looks like a small plastic part, but it sets the spacing, keeps cells from shifting and makes welding straight. Here is how to choose one.

What a holder does

Holders fix the cells at a set distance so the strip lines up with every terminal. They stop cells touching each other, give a little air gap, and reduce stress on welds when the pack is bumped. In compact packs they also make assembly faster and repeatable.

The 1X2 format holds two cells side by side. Stack them to build the rows your layout needs.

Match the strip to the holder

The spacing between cell centres decides which strip fits. Many of our 2P strips are made to suit a holder, for example the 21700 plated strip with holder. Read how this works in our centre distance guide.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Buying holders and strip separately without checking the spacing.
  • Mixing cell sizes in one holder row.
  • Leaving no room for vents or cooling in tightly packed rows.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What does 1X2 mean on a cell holder?

It describes the layout: one row of two cells. Join holders together to form larger blocks.

Do I need holders if I am spot-welding strip?

Not strictly, but they keep spacing even, make welding easier and give a more rigid pack. Most professional packs use them.

Can a holder replace glue or tape?

Holders position the cells but you still need the pack case or wrap to keep it together. Follow your pack design.
