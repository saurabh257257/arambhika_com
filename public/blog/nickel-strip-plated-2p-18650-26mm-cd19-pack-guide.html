2P Nickel Strip for 18650 Cells

The 26 mm CD 19 strip and its variants

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

18650 packs are the oldest and most common pack format. A 2P strip makes assembly fast and tidy.

The variants

StripNotes
Plated 26x0.15 mm CD 19The standard choice; centre distance 19 mm
Plated 26x0.15 mm without slitPlain strip with no slit cut
Pure 26x0.15 mmHigher conductivity for heavier loads
Pure zig-zag 26x0.30 mmThicker, formed shape for high-current layouts

How to choose

  • Light and moderate loads, cost first: plated.
  • High current or long life: pure, and thicker if your welder can manage it.
  • Unusual layouts: ask about the zig-zag.

Fit and holders

CD 19 matches an 18650 packed with a small gap. Use 18650 holders to hold that spacing. See the CD guide.

Weld tips

Run test welds on scrap cells and do a pull test. Our spot-welding guide covers the settings.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What does 26x0.15 mean?

The strip is 26 mm wide and 0.15 mm thick.

Is pure strip worth the extra cost?

For high-current packs, yes. For light loads plated is usually enough.

Can I cut 2P strip into 1P?

It can be cut, but the 2P form is designed to join two cells together as made.
