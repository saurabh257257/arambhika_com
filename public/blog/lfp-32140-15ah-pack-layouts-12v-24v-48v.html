32140 LFP Pack Layouts

12.8V, 25.6V and 51.2V from one cell

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

The 32140 LFP cell is 3.2 V and 15 Ah. Three layouts cover most 12 V, 24 V and 48 V needs.

Voltage from series, capacity from parallel

Each cell is 3.2 V nominal and 15 Ah. Cells in series add voltage. Cells in parallel add capacity. LFP cells are charged to 3.65 V each.

The 8S4P and 16S2P packs hold the same energy in different voltages. Pick the voltage your inverter, charger or motor controller needs, then size the parallel count for capacity.

Current limits

The datasheet of one model in our range, the CBAK 32140 15 Ah, gives a maximum charge of 1C (15 A) and a maximum discharge of 2C (45 A) per cell. With 4 cells in parallel that is 60 A charge and 180 A discharge at the pack level, but stay well below it for a long life. Always confirm the figures for the exact cell you buy.

Building it

  • Use cell holders to keep the cylinders aligned, and a spot-welded strip of the right size. See nickel strip for 32650 and 32140 cells.
  • Choose the BMS for the series count in the table, rated above your peak current.
  • Top-balance the cells before you join them.

Cells in this range include JSK 32140 ESS A grade and MOCEAN 32140. See also Build a 12.8V 60Ah 32140 Pack.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

How many 32140 cells make a 12V pack?

Four in series (4S) gives 12.8 V nominal. Add parallel groups for more capacity, for example 4S4P for 60 Ah.

What BMS do I need?

One with the same series count as your pack and an LFP charge voltage of 3.65 V per cell, rated above your peak current.

Can I use a lead-acid charger?

Not without checking it. LFP needs its own charge profile and a BMS to protect each cell.
