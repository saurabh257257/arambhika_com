Pure Nickel 2P Strip for 21700

CD 22.5 vs CD 23, and plated alternatives

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Two spacings (22.5 and 23) and both plated and pure versions make the 21700 range wide. Here is how to pick.

The line-up

MaterialCD 22.5CD 23
Pure, with holder31x0.15 mm31x0.15 mm
Plated, with holder31x0.15 mm, slit31x0.15 mm, slit
Plated, without holderwith slitwithout slit

Which CD?

Measure the centre-to-centre spacing in your layout or holder. A 21700 is about 21 mm wide, so a CD of 22.5 is a tight pack and 23 leaves a little more gap. The strip must match, otherwise weld points miss the terminal.

Pure or plated

Pure nickel conducts better so it runs cooler at the same size. It costs more per kg. Use pure for fast chargers, high-power builds and long-life packs; plated for ordinary use.

Frequently asked questions

Does CD 23 fit a CD 22.5 holder?

Not reliably. Match the strip to the holder spacing.

Is "with holder" strip only for holders?

It is made for holder-based layouts. Use it with the matching holder.

Can I weld pure nickel with a small welder?

Pure nickel conducts heat and current well, which can make welding need more energy than plated. Test welds first.
