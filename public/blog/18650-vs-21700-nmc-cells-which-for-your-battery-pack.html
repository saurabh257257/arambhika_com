18650 vs 21700 NMC Cells

Capacity, cell count and cost compared

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Both cells use similar chemistry, but a 21700 holds far more energy than an 18650. Fewer larger cells mean fewer welds and a simpler pack.

The numbers

1865021700
Typical capacity (our range)2600 mAh4500 mAh
Energy at 3.6 Vabout 9.4 Whabout 16.2 Wh
Cells for 1 kWhabout 107about 62
Welds and strip lengthMoreFewer

Our cells

  • 18650: DMEGC 2600 mAh and Norda 2600 mAh EV grade
  • 21700: DMEGC 4500 mAh and Norda 4500 mAh

How to decide

  • Choose 21700 when you want the most energy in the least space, with fewer parts to assemble.
  • Choose 18650 when you need a smaller or more flexible shape, or you already use 18650 tooling and strip.
  • Check the discharge rate in the datasheet, not only the capacity. A high-current pack may need more cells in parallel either way.

Matching strip and holders

Each size has its own strip and holder; see the plated strip guide and the cell holder guide. To build a full pack, follow the 13S 48V build.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 21700 always better than an 18650?

It holds more energy per cell, but the best choice depends on space, current needs and your tooling.

What does EV grade mean?

It indicates a cell offered for higher-rate vehicle use. Always read the datasheet for the actual continuous and peak current rating.

Can I mix 18650 and 21700 in one pack?

No. Different sizes need different holders and strip, and different cell types should not be mixed in a series string.
