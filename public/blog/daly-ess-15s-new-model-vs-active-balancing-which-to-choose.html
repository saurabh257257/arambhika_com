Daly ESS 15S: New Model or Active?

Two ESS boards for 48V storage packs

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Home storage packs tend to use the ESS family of Daly boards. There are three 15S options, and the right one depends on current and balancing needs.

The three 15S ESS boards

BoardNotes
ESS 15S 100A active balancingMoves charge between cells; good for large cells
ESS 15S 150A active balancingSame, with more current for bigger inverters
ESS 15S 100A new modelUpdated version of the 100A board; read the current specification

Picking by inverter

Take the inverter's rated DC input current at your battery voltage. A 3 kW inverter at 48 V draws about 62 A; 5 kW about 104 A. The 100A boards suit the first; the 150A board gives room for the second.

If you want 16S

For 51.2 V packs, see the ESS 16S 100A new model and our active balancing guide. Pair either with the cells in our 314Ah build.

Ask before you order

Specifications change between model generations. Send us your inverter model and we will confirm which board suits it.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What changed in the "new model"?

Ask us for the current specification sheet; revisions vary. We can tell you what applies to the stock we hold.

Is 15S or 16S better for solar?

Either, if your inverter supports the voltage range. 16S gives about 51.2 V and is common with 48 V class inverters.

Can I upgrade from 100A to 150A later?

You can swap the BMS if the series count matches and the wiring and cells can carry the current.
