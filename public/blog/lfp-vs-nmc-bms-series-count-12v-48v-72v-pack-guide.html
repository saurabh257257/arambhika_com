LFP vs NMC BMS: Pick the Series Count

From 4S to 30S, matched to your voltage

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

A BMS is built for one chemistry and one series count. Buy the wrong one and it will either refuse to work or protect at the wrong voltages. This guide turns your target pack voltage into the right "S" number.

Start from the cell voltage

An LFP cell is about 3.2 V nominal and an NMC cell about 3.6 V nominal. Multiply by the number of cells in series (the "S" count) to get the pack voltage. The BMS must match both the chemistry and that count.

Do not mix chemistries

An LFP BMS charges to a lower cut-off than an NMC BMS. Using an NMC board on LFP cells leaves charge unused, and an LFP board on NMC cells over-charges them, which is unsafe. Always buy the board that names your chemistry. For the full build steps see our 48V scooter pack guide.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

How many cells in series for a 48V LFP pack?

Most 48 V LFP packs use 15S (about 48 V nominal) or 16S (about 51.2 V nominal). Check what your charger and controller are designed for.

Can I use a 16S BMS on a 15S pack?

No. The BMS monitors each series group, so the cell count has to match the board.

Is 13S NMC the same as 48V?

A 13S NMC pack is about 46.8 V nominal and is sold as a 48 V pack. Fully charged it sits near 54.6 V.
