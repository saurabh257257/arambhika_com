Why a range?

A board that covers 7S to 24S can serve 24 V, 48 V and 72 V LFP builds from one stock item. That is useful for assemblers with mixed orders.

What to check

  • The range includes your series count (for example 16S lies inside 6S-22S).
  • The current rating covers your load with margin.
  • The setup method for cell count is clear in the manual.

Frequently asked questions

What does semi-smart include?

Features vary by model. Read the specification of the exact part you buy.

Which board for a 48V LFP pack?

Any whose range includes 15S or 16S and whose current covers the load, such as the 6S-22S or 7S-24S models.

Is 4S 100A semi-smart good for a 12V battery?

Yes for moderate loads. For very heavy 12 V loads see the high-current boards.
