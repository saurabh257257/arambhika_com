Pack Building

Large Epoxy Sheet 512x1225 mm

Cutting layout, yield and handling

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

If you cut a lot of insulation pieces, the large sheet gives you far more area for the money than the small ones.

Area comparison

The 512 x 1225 x 0.5 mm sheet covers about 0.63 m². The 502 x 338 x 0.5 mm sheet covers about 0.17 m². One large sheet is equal to about 3.7 small ones in area. Both are priced per kg.

Plan your cuts

  1. List every piece you cut: base plates, covers, separators.
  2. Lay the shapes on a drawing of the sheet and arrange them to leave little waste.
  3. Keep grain and thickness consistent across a pack.
  4. Cut the biggest pieces first, then fit the small ones in the offcuts.

Cutting and handling

  • Use a sharp blade or a fine saw; wear a dust mask and eye protection.
  • Deburr edges so they do not nick wires.
  • Store flat and dry, away from sunlight.

When to choose the small sheets

For a few thicker pieces, the 2 mm or 1 mm sheets give you the stiffness. Read the thickness guide.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Is the large sheet only 0.5 mm?

This listing is 0.5 mm. Other thicknesses come in the smaller 502x338 mm size.

Can I fold it?

It is a rigid board. Cut and fit flat pieces rather than bending.

How do I know how much I need?

Add up the area of all your pieces and add 15 to 20 percent for waste.
