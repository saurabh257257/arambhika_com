Pack Building

Battery Pack Bill of Materials

A checklist of every part, from cells to potting

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 6 min read

Forgetting one small part stalls a build. Use this checklist to gather everything before you start.

1. Cells

Choose by chemistry and size: NMC 21700 or 18650 for cylindrical packs, LFP 32140 for round LFP, or prismatic LFP for storage and vehicles. Always buy a full pack's worth from one batch.

2. Connections

  • Spot-welded cylindrical packs: plated strip or pure nickel strip
  • Prismatic packs: copper busbar or aluminium busbar

3. Structure

Cell holders for cylindrical cells, plus an enclosure that suits your product.

4. Protection

A BMS matched to chemistry, series count and current, plus a main fuse or breaker sized to the load.

5. Insulation and sealing

Epoxy sheet for barriers and base plates, and potting compound for electronics that face water or vibration.

6. Tools

  • Spot welder for strip (not needed for bolted busbar)
  • Torque wrench for busbar bolts
  • Multimeter, balance charger or tester
  • Insulated tools, gloves and eye protection

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a spot welder for every pack?

Only for strip-welded cylindrical packs. Prismatic cells use bolted or laser-welded busbar, so no spot welder is needed.

What is the part people forget most?

The main fuse and insulation. A BMS alone is not a replacement for a correctly rated fuse and clean insulation.

Can I buy everything from one place?

Yes. Cells, strip, busbar, holders, BMS, epoxy sheet and potting compound are all on our store.
