BMS for 24V Inverter and UPS

8S LFP boards for 25.6V home backup

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Many home inverters run on 24 V. An 8S LFP pack is a common lithium replacement for a pair of lead-acid batteries.

The load sum

A 1 kVA inverter draws about 40 A at 25.6 V (1000 / 25.6 is roughly 39), and a 2 kVA about 78 A. A 100 A board covers both with some margin.

Your 8S options at 100A

  • Daly LFP 8S 100A hardware
  • JBD 8S 100A LFP hardware, inverter version
  • JBD 8S-24S 100A smart if you want monitoring

Cells for the pack

Eight prismatic cells of 100Ah to 230Ah in series; see the 230Ah GreatPower and the 100Ah and 150Ah guide. 8S with 100Ah is about 2.6 kWh.

Inverter checks

  • Set the inverter's battery type to lithium or user-defined with LFP voltages.
  • Make sure the charger stops at the right voltage for 8S LFP.
  • Fit a DC breaker or fuse sized for the current.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Can I replace two 12V lead-acid batteries with an 8S pack?

Two 12 V in series make 24 V, so an 8S LFP pack can replace them if the inverter and charger support lithium.

Is 8S 25.6V or 24V?

About 25.6 V nominal. It is sold as a 24 V pack.

Smart or hardware for a UPS?

Smart helps you see state of charge; hardware is cheaper and simple. Either protects the pack.
