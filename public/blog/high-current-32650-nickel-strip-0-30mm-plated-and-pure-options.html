0.30 mm Nickel Strip for 32650

When 0.15 mm is not enough

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

A pack that moves serious current, like an e-rickshaw or a heavy inverter, may outgrow 0.15 mm strip.

The 0.30 mm options

StripNotes
Plated 47x0.30 CD 32.8, no holder, no slitTight spacing, plain strip
Plated 46.5x0.30 CD 32.8, no holder, slitSame spacing, slit version
Plated 47x0.30 CD 34.5, holder, slitHolder layout, slit
Pure 47x0.30 CD 34.5Best conductivity

Why thicker

Doubling thickness doubles cross-section. That cuts resistance and heat at the joint, which matters when many amps flow. Pure nickel adds conductivity on top.

But check three things

  1. Your welder can weld 0.30 mm reliably; see the LFP welding guide.
  2. Your layout and holder match the CD (32.8 or 34.5).
  3. The cells themselves can supply the current.

Alternatives

For very high current, a bolted copper busbar design may suit better than strip. Read the thickness guide.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Is 0.30 mm needed for LFP storage?

Often not. Storage packs run lower currents; 0.15 to 0.20 mm may be enough.

Does thicker strip run cooler?

At the same current, yes: larger cross-section means less resistance.

Which holder fits CD 34.5?

Use a holder with the matching spacing; check the cell holder listing or ask us.
