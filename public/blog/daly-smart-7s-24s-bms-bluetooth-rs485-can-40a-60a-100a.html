BMS Guide

Daly Smart 7S-24S BMS

One board for many pack sizes, with comms

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

This board is built for flexibility: one hardware family that works across many cell counts, with a balancer and communication options on board.

What is on the board

  • An inbuilt balancer to keep cells in step.
  • Bluetooth for phone monitoring and settings.
  • RS485 and CAN ports for talking to an inverter, charger or display.

Three current ratings

RatingProduct

Good fits

Packs from roughly 24 V to the high-voltage end of the range, for inverters with CAN or RS485 support, for fleets that want one spare board type, and for builders who change pack sizes often.

Before you buy

Confirm the chemistry setting you need, the series count and whether your inverter speaks the protocol this board offers. Read about active balancing boards if your cells are large.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with LFP and NMC?

Check the product specification for the chemistries supported and how to select them.

Will CAN work with any inverter?

No. The inverter must support the protocol the BMS uses. Check both manuals.

Is the balancer active or passive?

Read the specification of the model you pick. The ESS range is the one with active balancing.
