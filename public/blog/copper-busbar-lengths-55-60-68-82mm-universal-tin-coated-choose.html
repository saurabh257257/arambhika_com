Copper Busbar: 55, 60, 68 or 82 mm

Match the bar to your cell spacing

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

The four universal bars share the same 20 mm width and 2 mm thickness. Only the length changes, so choosing is about spacing.

The four lengths

BarCross-section
55 x 20 x 2 mm40 mm²
60 x 20 x 2 mm40 mm²
68 x 20 x 2 mm40 mm²
82 x 20 x 2 mm40 mm²

All share a 40 mm² cross-section (20 x 2), so current capacity is similar; only reach differs.

Pick by terminal spacing

  1. Measure from the centre of one terminal to the centre of the next.
  2. Choose a bar that is longer than that distance by the hole margins at each end.
  3. Drill or confirm the hole positions to fit the cell terminal bolt size.

Using more than one bar in parallel

For more current, use two bars side by side across the same terminals, or step up to a bar of larger section. Always size for your continuous and peak current and check temperature on a test run.

Frequently asked questions

Are the bars pre-drilled?

Check the product page and photos for hole details. Ask us if your cell needs a particular hole pattern.

Why is copper tin-plated?

Tin helps resist oxidation and gives a stable surface for bolted joints.

Can I bend the bar?

Copper can be bent gently, but bending can crack the tin coating. Use a straight bar where possible.
