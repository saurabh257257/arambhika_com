Pack Building

Holders for 26650, 33140, 34145

Larger cylindrical cells need their own holders

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Beyond 18650 and 21700, bigger cylindrical cells appear in LFP and high-capacity packs. Holders for them are harder to find.

The three sizes

  • 26650 holder for cells 26 mm in diameter, common in LFP packs.
  • 33140 holder for 33 mm cells, 140 mm long.
  • 34145 holder for 34 mm cells, 145 mm long.

Why holders matter more with big cells

Larger cells are heavier, so a loose cell can strain its weld during vibration. A holder carries the load and keeps the spacing right for strip.

Checklist

  1. Confirm your cell dimensions against the holder size; maker tolerances vary.
  2. Stack holders to build rows and interlock them as designed.
  3. Match strip spacing; see the 32650 strip guide.

Related

For 18650 to 32650 see the main holder guide. If your cell is not listed, tell us the model and we will check what holder fits.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What cells are 33140?

Cylindrical cells about 33 mm wide and 140 mm long, used in some LFP packs.

Can one holder fit two sizes?

Not reliably. Use the holder made for your cell size.

Do holders improve cooling?

They set a gap between cells, which helps air flow if the pack case allows it.
