Build a 24V 230Ah LFP Battery

8S x 230Ah, BMS and busbar parts list

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 6 min read

Eight 230Ah cells make a 25.6 V battery of about 5.9 kWh, a good size for a 24 V inverter that serves a small home.

The maths

8 x 3.2 V = 25.6 V. Energy is 25.6 V x 230 Ah = 5,888 Wh, about 5.9 kWh nominal. A 2 kVA inverter at 25.6 V draws about 78 A, so a 100 A BMS gives headroom.

Parts list

  • 8 cells: GreatPower 230Ah, same batch
  • BMS: Daly 8S 100A hardware or JBD 8S-24S 100A smart
  • Busbar: copper bars or aluminium C2C series bars
  • Insulation: epoxy sheet, plus fuse and enclosure

Build steps

  1. Measure and match cell voltages; parallel-balance them.
  2. Stack with the recommended compression, with insulation where needed.
  3. Join cells in series and torque the bolts evenly.
  4. Wire the BMS; follow the wiring guide.
  5. Test with a low load before connecting the inverter.

Inverter settings

Set the inverter to lithium or custom voltages for 8S LFP. See the 24 V BMS guide.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours can 5.9 kWh run a 500W load?

Roughly 10 to 11 hours, allowing for inverter loss and a safe depth of discharge. Real figures vary.

Can I expand later?

Add a second identical battery in parallel through a proper breaker setup, with matched voltage; ask a qualified installer.

Do 230Ah cells need compression?

Prismatic cells are generally compressed within the range the cell maker specifies.
