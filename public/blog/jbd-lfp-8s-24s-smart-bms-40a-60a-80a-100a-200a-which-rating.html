JBD 8S-24S Smart BMS: Which Rating?

From 40A to 200A on one platform

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

The same board family runs from 40 A to 200 A, which helps when you build packs of different power levels.

What each rating supports

Rating25.6 V (8S)51.2 V (16S)73.6 V (23S)
40 Aabout 1.0 kWabout 2.0 kWabout 2.9 kW
60 Aabout 1.5 kWabout 3.1 kWabout 4.4 kW
80 Aabout 2.0 kWabout 4.1 kWabout 5.9 kW
100 Aabout 2.6 kWabout 5.1 kWabout 7.4 kW
200 Aabout 5.1 kWabout 10.2 kWabout 14.7 kW

These are nominal voltage times rated current. Real limits depend on cooling and the cells.

Choosing

  • 40A to 60A for e-bikes, small backup and light vehicles.
  • 80A to 100A for mid-size inverters and scooters.
  • 200A for big inverter banks, low-voltage heavy loads and high-power vehicles.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use an 8S-24S board for NMC?

This listing is for LFP. For NMC use a board made for NMC, as the voltage limits differ.

Is 200A enough for a 5 kW inverter at 12V?

A 5 kW load at 12.8 V is about 390 A, so 200A is not enough. Use a higher-voltage battery.

Do bigger BMS get hot?

Any BMS warms under load. Mount it with airflow and keep cables short.
