Daly NMC 20S BMS for 72V Packs

A 72V NMC pack from 20 cells in series

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Twenty NMC cells in series give 72 V nominal, around 84 V when full. It is a common voltage for faster scooters and light electric motorcycles.

Power at 40A

72 V times 40 A is about 2.9 kW. That suits a mid-power scooter. Heavier motors may need a higher-current approach, such as the JBD NMC 20S 50A, or a different pack design. The Daly choice here is the NMC 20S 40A smart BMS.

Cell count

20S with four cells in parallel is 80 cells (20S4P). With 4500 mAh cells such as DMEGC 21700 that is 18 Ah, about 1.3 kWh. Add strip, holders and insulation from the bill of materials.

Voltage safety

At 84 V full charge, treat the pack as high voltage. Use insulated tools and fit a fuse on the main positive. Keep the spot-welded groups separated until you are ready to join the series string.

Charger match

Choose a 20S NMC charger with the correct end voltage. Compare with the LFP and NMC series-count table before ordering anything.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What is the full-charge voltage of a 20S NMC pack?

About 84 V, at 4.2 V per cell.

Is 40A enough for my scooter?

Compare it with your controller's battery current limit. Choose a higher-rated BMS if the limit is above 40 A.

Can I use a 24S LFP BMS?

No. Use a BMS made for the chemistry and number of cells in your pack.
