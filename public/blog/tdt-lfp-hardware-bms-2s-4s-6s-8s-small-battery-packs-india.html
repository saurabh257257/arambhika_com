BMS Guide

TDT LFP BMS for Small Packs

Low-current boards for 6.4V to 25.6V LFP packs

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Not every battery is for a vehicle. Lamps, speakers, solar lights and tools use small LFP packs, and TDT makes boards sized for them.

Pick by series count

  • 2S (6.4 V): 2S 4A, for very small packs.
  • 4S (12.8 V): 6A, 8A, 10A, 20A, 40A and 100
  • 6S (19.2 V): 6S 10A
  • 8S (25.6 V): 8S 30A

Sizing

Take the load in watts, divide by pack voltage, and add margin. A 100 W LED floodlight on 12.8 V draws about 8 A, so a 10A or 20A board fits. Our sizing guide has more examples.

Good uses

  • Solar street lights and garden lamps
  • Portable speakers and power stations
  • Small UPS and router backups

Cells for small packs

Pair them with LFP cylindrical cells, for example the 32140 15Ah, joined with suitable strip.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Do the colour variants (blue, green) differ?

They are different board versions. Ask us which suits your wiring if you are unsure.

Can a 4S board protect a 3S NMC pack?

No. Use a board made for the chemistry and cell count of your pack.

Are these good for e-bikes?

No. E-bike loads need higher-current boards such as the Daly or JBD ranges.
