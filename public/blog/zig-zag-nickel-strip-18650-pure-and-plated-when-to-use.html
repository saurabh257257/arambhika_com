Zig-Zag Nickel Strip for 18650

Pure and plated formed strip explained

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Most strip is straight. Zig-zag strip is stamped into a repeating shape for pack layouts that need it.

The three zig-zag strips

  • Plated 2P(18650) zig-zag 0.15 mm
  • Pure 2P(18650) zig-zag 19.01x0.15 mm
  • Pure 2P(18650) zig-zag 26x0.30 mm

Where a formed shape helps

Because the strip follows the layout of the cells, it can connect staggered or alternating rows with less cutting. It also gives repeatable weld positions, which helps when you build many identical packs.

Choosing among the three

NeedPick
Lowest cost, light loadsPlated 0.15 mm
Better conductivity, same thicknessPure 0.15 mm
High currentPure 0.30 mm, with a welder that can handle it

Fit check first

Specifications such as spacing differ between these formed strips and the standard 2P strip. Before ordering in bulk, send us a photo or drawing of your layout. Compare with the standard 18650 strips in our 18650 guide.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Does zig-zag strip weld differently?

The welding is the same process. Check that your electrodes reach every weld spot in the pattern.

Is zig-zag stronger than flat strip?

The shape mainly helps layout and spacing. Current capacity still depends on cross-section.
