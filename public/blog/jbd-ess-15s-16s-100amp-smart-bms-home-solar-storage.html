JBD ESS BMS for Home Solar

15S and 16S boards for storage batteries

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

ESS stands for energy storage system. These boards are meant for stationary batteries that cycle daily and run for years.

The JBD ESS boards

  • JBD ESS 15S 100A smart for about 48 V packs
  • JBD ESS 16S 100A (UP 16S019-K V1.2) for about 51.2 V
  • JBD ESS 16S 100A (UP1S026-K V 1.2), a second 16S variant

Why two 16S boards?

They carry different part codes, so features such as ports and communication may differ. Check which one lists your inverter's protocol, or send us the inverter model and we will help you choose.

What to verify

  1. Series count matches your cell string (15S or 16S).
  2. 100 A covers your inverter's DC current: a 5 kW inverter at 51.2 V draws about 98 A.
  3. The communication port suits your inverter.

Also consider

If your cells are large, an active balancing board may help. For the cell side of the build, follow the 314Ah storage build.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Is an ESS BMS different from a normal BMS?

It is designed for stationary storage, often with communication ports for inverters and larger cells in mind.

Which JBD 16S board should I pick?

Check the inverter protocol list for each part code, or message us with your inverter model.

Can I use it for a vehicle?

It is intended for storage. For vehicles, use a BMS made for traction loads.
