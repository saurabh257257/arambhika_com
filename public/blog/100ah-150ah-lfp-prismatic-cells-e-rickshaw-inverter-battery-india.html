100Ah and 150Ah LFP Prismatic Cells

Where mid-size cells are the right fit

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Not every project needs a 314Ah cell. Mid-size cells give flexible pack voltages and capacities for vehicles and smaller backup systems.

Why choose 100Ah or 150Ah

  • Smaller cells let you shape voltage and capacity with less over-sizing.
  • They are easier to carry and fit into tight vehicle battery boxes.
  • A series-parallel layout lets you reach the capacity you need from small increments.

Pair the pack with a BMS matched to the series count; see the series count guide. For vehicles, plan secure mounting and read our e-rickshaw pack guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can 100Ah cells be paralleled?

Yes, for more capacity. Match the cells, connect them with equal-length busbar, and use a BMS rated for the total current.

Are LFP cells a drop-in for a lead-acid battery?

A 4S LFP pack is about 12.8 V, which suits many 12 V systems, but the charger and any alternator must support lithium charging.
