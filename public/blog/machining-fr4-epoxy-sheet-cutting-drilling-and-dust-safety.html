Cutting FR-4 Epoxy Sheet

Tools, dust control and a clean finish

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

FR-4 cuts well, but it is glass-filled. That dulls tools quickly and the dust is not something to breathe.

Protect yourself first

Cutting or sanding FR-4 makes fine dust containing glass fibre and cured resin. It irritates skin, eyes and lungs.

  • Wear a dust mask rated for fine particles, eye protection and gloves.
  • Cut where dust is pulled away, with a vacuum or extraction at the tool. Do not blow it off with air.
  • Clean up with a vacuum, or a damp cloth, not a dry brush.
  • Keep it away from food and wash your hands after.

Choosing the tool

JobTool
Straight cuts, thin sheetFine-tooth saw, or a guillotine shear for thin sheet
Straight cuts, thicker sheetCircular or jig saw with a carbide-tipped or diamond blade
HolesSharp carbide drill bits, with the sheet clamped and backed
Shapes and slotsA router or CNC with a carbide cutter

Ordinary steel bits and blades dull fast on glass-filled material. Plan on carbide or diamond for any volume.

Getting a clean edge

  1. Mark the line, and support the sheet close to the cut so it does not flex or chip.
  2. Clamp it down. A vibrating sheet cracks and chips along the cut.
  3. Feed steadily and do not force the tool. Let the cutter work.
  4. Put a scrap sheet underneath when drilling, so the exit side of the hole does not break out.
  5. Deburr edges and hole rims lightly with fine sandpaper, and wipe clean.

Check the result

Look for chipped edges and hairline cracks, particularly near holes. A sheet with a crack running to the edge should not be used as an insulating part, since it can propagate. Keep cut sheet dry and clean before assembly.

Choose thickness first with our thickness guide. Sheets: 502 x 338 x 1 mm and the large 512 x 1225 x 0.5 mm.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a mask to cut FR-4?

Yes. The dust contains glass fibre. Use a suitable mask, eye protection and extraction.

Can I cut it with a normal wood blade?

You can, but it will dull quickly. A carbide-tipped or diamond blade lasts far longer.

Why does the hole edge chip?

The drill breaks out of the back face. Back the sheet with a scrap board and feed slowly.
