Kapton Tape for Battery Packs

Where polyimide tape earns its place

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

Kapton is the brand name for polyimide film tape. It is thin, strong and handles heat that would melt ordinary tapes.

Why it is used

Polyimide film is a good electrical insulator, stays stable at high temperatures and stays thin. In a battery pack those three qualities matter, because space is tight and parts near welds and solder joints get hot. Check the datasheet of your roll for its voltage and temperature ratings before you rely on it for a specific job.

Good places to use it

  • Covering strip ends and cut edges so they cannot touch the next cell or the case.
  • Insulating between the top of the cells and a BMS or board that sits on the pack.
  • Protecting wires and solder joints near welds and soldering points.
  • Masking and holding parts during soldering, since it tolerates the heat.
  • Insulating the weld area of a joint you have finished and inspected.

Where it is not the best choice

  • Strapping and holding cells together. It is thin and not made for tension. Use filament tape.
  • Mounting a board. Use double-sided tape.
  • Large wrapping jobs. It costs more per metre than PVC tape, so save it for the jobs that need it.

Using it well

  1. Clean and dry the surface first.
  2. Apply it flat, pressing out air bubbles, and overlap each turn by a few millimetres.
  3. Fold the tape over edges, and do not leave corners lifting.
  4. Keep it clear of weld spots, because it can get in the way of electrodes.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kapton tape a brand or a material?

Kapton is a brand of polyimide film. Tapes of this kind are often called Kapton tape.

Can it replace all other tapes?

No. It is excellent for heat and insulation, but not for strapping or mounting.

Which width should I choose?

Use 25 mm for narrow jobs such as strip ends and wires, and 50 mm for larger surfaces.
