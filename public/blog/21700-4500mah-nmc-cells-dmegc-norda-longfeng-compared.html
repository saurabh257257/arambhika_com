Same size, same capacity, different makers. The datasheet shows where they differ.

The three cells

What to compare

ItemLook for
Continuous discharge currentNeeded amps per cell = pack peak current / cells in parallel
Internal resistanceLower reduces heat and voltage sag
Batch consistencyVoltage and resistance across the batch
Price per WhLive price divided by about 16.2 Wh

Per-cell current example

A 13S4P e-bike pack drawing 40 A peak puts 10 A on each cell. Check the cell's rated continuous current is above that with a margin. If not, add parallel cells.

Buying advice

Choose one make and buy all the cells from one batch. Pair with 21700 holders and 2P strip. Full build: 13S 21700 pack.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.
