Build a 51.2V 314Ah Storage Battery

Cells, BMS and busbar for a 16 kWh pack

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 6 min read

The 314Ah prismatic LFP cell has become the standard building block for home and small commercial storage. Sixteen in series gives a 51.2 V battery that suits most 48 V class inverters.

The capacity maths

One cell is 3.2 V nominal. Sixteen in series (16S) gives 51.2 V. At 314 Ah, that is about 51.2 x 314 = 16,077 Wh, or roughly 16 kWh of nominal energy. Usable energy is a little less once you leave margin at the top and bottom of the charge.

Choosing the cells

We list 314Ah cells from several makers, including Highstar, GreatPower and Ganfeng (laser weld). Buy all 16 cells from one batch, with matching voltage and internal resistance, so the pack balances well. For smaller systems, see the CALB 280Ah and GreatPower 230Ah options.

The rest of the bill of materials

  • BMS: a 16S board such as the Daly ESS 16S 100A smart BMS. See why in our active balancing guide.
  • Busbar: copper or aluminium links sized for the inverter current.
  • Insulation: epoxy sheet between the cell block and the case.
  • Fuse, main breaker and a rigid enclosure.

Assembly notes

  1. Check each cell's voltage. Top-balance them in parallel before building the series string.
  2. Compress the cells within the range the cell maker specifies, since LFP cells swell with use.
  3. Torque busbar bolts evenly and record the values.
  4. Charge and discharge slowly for the first cycles while watching cell voltages on the BMS app.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

How many kWh is a 16S 314Ah pack?

About 16 kWh nominal (51.2 V x 314 Ah). Usable energy depends on the depth of discharge you allow.

Do I need to compress prismatic cells?

Cell makers usually specify a compression range to keep cells flat over life. Follow the datasheet for the exact cell you buy.

Can I build a 24V or 12V pack from 314Ah cells?

Yes, with 8S (25.6 V) or 4S (12.8 V). Current rises as voltage falls, so check the BMS and busbar rating.
