How Many Cell Holders?

Holder count for any cylindrical pack

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

A 1x2 holder holds two cells side by side. Counting how many you need takes one division.

The rule

Each 1x2 holder carries two cells, so the number of holders is the number of cells in the pack divided by two, rounded up. An odd cell count leaves one holder with a single cell. That is fine, but it is worth planning for.

Worked examples

13S5P, 217006533 (one holds a single cell)

Not sure how many cells your pack needs? Use the cell count calculator first.

Match the holder to the cell

The holder is made for one cell size. Our 1x2 holders cover six cylindrical formats: 18650, 21700, 26650, 32650, 33140 and 34145. A holder for the wrong size will not hold cells properly, so check your cell's diameter and length first. Cells such as 32140 LFP are a different size from the formats listed, so ask us if the holder suits your cell.

Minimum orders

Holders are sold per piece with a minimum order that depends on the size, from 100 to 500 pieces. Count what you need for a batch of packs and add a few percent for breakage and test builds.

What the holder does

The holder keeps cells in place, cuts vibration and leaves a gap between cells that helps heat escape. It does not replace the insulation between cells and strip, and it does not hold the cells to the case, so plan for both. See Cell Holders for Battery Packs for more.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.
