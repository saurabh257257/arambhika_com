Battery Insulation Paper Rings

Why they matter and how to fit them

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

A cylindrical cell has bare metal at the edge of its terminal. A paper ring is the cheapest protection against a short there.

What the ring does

Insulation paper is a die-cut ring, sized for one cell format. It is fitted to the cell as a barrier between the terminal region and nearby conductive pack hardware, such as the strip on the next group, a case or a bracket. Without it, a small shift or a burr can short a cell to something it should not touch.

Sizes

The ring is made for a particular cell size, so choose by your cell format:

  • 18650, supplied in production packs (the listing is a 10,000-piece pack)
  • 21700
  • 26650
  • 32700
  • 33140

Check the pack size on each product page, because the number of rings in a pack differs by listing.

Fitting it

  1. Fit the rings before you lay the strip, with the cells already in their holders, as in our assembly order.
  2. Seat each ring flat and centred on the cell end, so the contact area for the weld stays clear.
  3. Make sure the ring does not cover the weld spots. A ring in the way will weaken the weld.
  4. Fix it with the cell holder and the strip, or with a dot of suitable adhesive, so it does not slide.

Handling and storage

  • Keep the paper dry. Moisture reduces its insulation.
  • Store the pack sealed, and take out only what you need.
  • Do not reuse a ring that is creased, torn or oily.

A note on quantity

You need one ring per cell, plus a few percent for waste. Count the cells with our cell count calculator.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

How many rings do I need?

One for each cell, plus a few percent extra for mistakes.

Does the ring go on the positive or negative end?

Follow your pack design and the product listing. Our 18650 listing is for the negative terminal end, and the product page for each size says how it is used.

Will the ring stop a weld working?

Only if it covers the weld spot. Keep the weld area clear.
