BMS Guide

TDT NMC BMS for 1S to 6S Packs

Protection boards for small Li-ion packs

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

A single 18650 or a small series pack still needs protection. TDT boards cover 1S to 6S for everyday DIY and repair.

What the names mean

  • 1S: one cell, such as 1S 2A (round) or 1S 3A with NTC.
  • 2S: two cells in series, 7.4 V nominal, 2S 4A.
  • 3S: 11.1 V nominal, in 5A, 10A and 20A.
  • 6S: 22.2 V nominal, 6S 10A.

Balancing and NTC

An NTC is a temperature sensor that lets the board react to heat. Balancing keeps series cells in step. See the 3S 10A with balancing and without balancing versions; choose balancing for packs you will cycle often.

Good uses

  • Power banks and small speakers
  • Cordless tool battery repairs; see the power tool pack guide
  • Torch, lamp and hobby projects

Cells and strip

Use matched 18650 cells and spot-welded nickel strip. Never solder directly onto cells.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Can I charge a 3S pack through the same board?

Most small boards charge and discharge through shared terminals. Check the wiring diagram for yours.

Why does NTC matter?

It lets the board sense temperature and cut off if the pack gets too hot or cold, where supported.

What charger does a 3S pack need?

A 3S Li-ion charger at 12.6 V full charge, with current suited to your cells.
