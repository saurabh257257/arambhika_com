Silicone Wire Gauge Guide

AWG to mm2, resistance and voltage drop

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

Silicone wire is flexible and stands up to heat, which makes it the favourite for battery leads. The gauge decides how much power you waste in it.

The four sizes

Copper has a resistivity of about 1.72 x 10-8 ohm metre, so resistance per metre is that figure divided by the cross-section.

GaugeCross-sectionResistance per metre, about
4 AWG21.2 mm20.81 milliohm
7 AWG10.6 mm21.63 milliohm
10 AWG5.26 mm23.27 milliohm
16 AWG1.31 mm213.1 milliohm

Worked example: 20 A over a 2 m round trip

For a lead that is 1 m each way, the total length is 2 m. Voltage drop is current times resistance, and the heat in the wire is current squared times resistance.

GaugeResistance (2 m)Voltage drop at 20 AHeat in the wire
4 AWG1.6 milliohm0.03 V0.7 W
7 AWG3.3 milliohm0.07 V1.3 W
10 AWG6.5 milliohm0.13 V2.6 W
16 AWG26 milliohm0.52 V10.5 W

On a 12.8 V pack, 0.52 V is about 4 percent of the voltage, and 10 W of heat in a thin wire is a lot. The same load on 10 AWG loses about 1 percent. This is why thin wire is for balance leads and sensors, and thick wire is for the main current path.

Choosing for your pack

  1. Find the continuous current and the length of the run (out and back).
  2. Pick a gauge that keeps the voltage drop to a small share of the pack voltage, and the heat low.
  3. Check the wire datasheet for the current it is rated for at your temperature and bundling, since heat is the other limit.
  4. Make sure the fuse protects the wire, so it is rated lower than the wire's safe current.

Frequently asked questions

What is AWG?

American Wire Gauge. A smaller number means a thicker wire. 4 AWG is much thicker than 16 AWG.

Why does thin wire get hot?

A thin wire has more resistance, so more of the current turns into heat.

Which gauge for a 12 V, 40 A load?

Work out the voltage drop and the heat for your run length first. For a short run, 7 AWG or thicker is a common starting point, then check the wire datasheet.
