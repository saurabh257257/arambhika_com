Hardware BMS boards are simple, cheaper protection for packs that do not need monitoring. Daly sells them across many series counts.

What a hardware BMS does and does not do

It protects against over-charge, over-discharge, over-current and short circuit with fixed thresholds. It has no app. If you want to see cell voltages, choose a smart board.

Choosing the rating

Use the controller's current limit with margin; our rating guide has worked examples. Two common 4S boards also exist: the 4S 100A hardware for 12 V packs.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What does the "IH" in some model names mean?

We list a few IH variants; ask us on WhatsApp which one fits your application and wiring before ordering.

Does a hardware BMS balance cells?

Most do basic balancing. Check the specification for the board you choose.

Can I swap a hardware BMS for a smart one later?

Yes, if the series count and current match. The wiring is similar.
