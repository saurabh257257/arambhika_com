PVC Sleeve for Battery Packs

How to choose 320, 340 or 360 mm

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

PVC sleeve wraps a finished pack in a tight, insulating skin. The one choice that matters is the width.

What the size means

Our PVC heat shrink sleeve is sold in rolls by size: 320, 340 and 360 mm. Sleeve for pack wrapping is normally sized by its lay-flat width, which is the width of the sleeve pressed flat. The sleeve is a tube, so the distance around it is twice the lay-flat width. If you are not sure how a size is measured, ask us before you order a roll.

Choose by the pack's cross-section

Measure the width (a) and height (b) of the pack at its widest cross-section, with strip and tape included. The distance around the pack is 2 x (a + b), so the sleeve needs a lay-flat width of at least (a + b), and a little more so that it slides over easily.

  • As a common rule, pick a lay-flat width about 5 to 10 percent larger than (a + b).
  • Sleeve far larger than the pack will not shrink tight and will wrinkle. Sleeve too small will not go on.

A worked example

A pack measures 150 x 140 mm in cross-section. a + b = 290 mm. Adding 5 to 10 percent gives 305 to 320 mm, so the 320 mm sleeve is the right choice. For a pack of 160 x 170 mm (a + b = 330), the 340 or 360 mm sleeve fits.

Applying it

  1. Cut the sleeve a little longer than the pack, so it can fold over the ends.
  2. Slide it over the pack, with the wires and terminals out of the way.
  3. Shrink it with a heat gun on a low or medium setting, keeping the gun moving. Work from the middle outward.
  4. Keep the heat away from cell terminals, plastic parts and wires, and do not let one spot get very hot.
  5. Check that the sleeve is snug and that no sharp edge pokes through it.

Rolls: 320 mm, 340 mm and 360 mm, sold per kg. Cover sharp strip ends with tape first, as in our tape guide.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What is lay-flat width?

The width of the sleeve when it is pressed flat. The distance around the tube is twice that.

Can I shrink it with a hairdryer?

A hairdryer is usually too weak. A heat gun on a low or medium setting does it properly, with care.

Does the sleeve replace insulation paper?

No. It is an outer wrap. Use insulation paper and tape inside the pack where parts need a barrier.
