Daly 23S BMS: 40A, 60A or 100A?

Right-sizing a 72V LFP pack BMS

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

A 23S LFP pack is about 73.6 V, the common 72 V class. Daly sells it in several ratings and in both smart and hardware form.

Power each rating supports

RatingApprox. power at 73.6 VTypical use
40 Aabout 2.9 kWLight scooters and small loaders
60 Aabout 4.4 kWMid-power scooters and 3-wheelers
100 Aabout 7.4 kWHeavy 3-wheelers and cargo

Models available

  • 23S 40A hardware
  • 23S 60A hardware and 23S 60A smart
  • 23S 100A hardware and 23S 100A smart

Smart or hardware?

For fleet vehicles, the smart version lets a technician read cell voltages over Bluetooth without opening the pack. For cost-sensitive builds, the hardware version protects the pack without extra features. See the comparison.

Use the controller's limit

Take the controller's battery current limit, add margin, and pick the next rating up. Read the e-rickshaw pack guide for the wider build.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Is 23S the same as 72V?

About. 23 LFP cells give 73.6 V nominal, sold as a 72 V pack.

Can I use a 20S NMC BMS on LFP cells?

No. NMC and LFP boards use different voltage limits. Buy the BMS for your chemistry and series count.

What if my motor needs more than 100A?

Move to a higher-rated BMS, or raise pack voltage so the same power needs less current.
