Cell Holders

Cylindrical Pack Assembly Order

From holders to welded strip, step by step

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

Getting the order right avoids rework. This is a sequence many pack builders follow, with a check at each stage.

Before you start

  • Have a drawing of the layout with polarity marked for every cell.
  • Check every cell for voltage and damage on arrival, and match them within the pack.
  • Work on a clean, dry, non-conductive surface, and remove rings and metal jewellery.

The sequence

  1. Fit the holders. Snap cells into cell holders in the layout you drew. Check the polarity of every cell against the drawing before going on.
  2. Add insulation paper. Fit the die-cut insulation, such as insulation paper for 18650 or for 21700, at the end of the cell where it keeps the terminal region apart from nearby conductive parts.
  3. Lay the strip. Place the nickel strip on the terminals. Choose the strip for your cell format and current. See sizing nickel strip.
  4. Test-weld. Make a few welds on a spare cell and pull-test them. Adjust the welder until the strip tears before the weld fails.
  5. Weld the pack. Follow our spot-welding guide. Weld in a pattern that does not heat one cell too long.
  6. Inspect. Look at every weld and tug each strip lightly. Re-weld weak spots.
  7. Check voltages. Measure each series group and compare. A group that reads off points to a wrong orientation or a bad weld.
  8. Connect the BMS. Wire the balance leads in order, following the BMS manual. See how to wire a BMS.
  9. Insulate and wrap. Cover bare strip ends and sharp edges, then wrap the pack with tape or sleeve.
  10. Test. Charge and discharge at a low current first, and check for heat at the welds and the BMS.

Common slips

  • A cell in backwards. Check polarity before welding, not after.
  • A strip that bridges two groups that should not touch. Look for contact at the edges.
  • Skipping the test welds, then finding weak welds in the finished pack.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Do I fit insulation before or after the holders?

Fit the holders first so the cells are in place, then add the insulation paper where your design needs it.

Why test-weld first?

Settings vary with the strip, the cell and the welder. A pull test on a spare cell shows if the welds are strong before you weld the real pack.

Which tape or sleeve do I use at the end?
