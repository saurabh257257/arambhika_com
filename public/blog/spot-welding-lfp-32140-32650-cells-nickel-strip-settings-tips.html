Spot-Welding LFP 32140 and 32650

Bigger cells, thicker strip, same careful method

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Large cylindrical LFP cells have thick cases and a lot of metal to heat. The method is the same as for 18650s, with a few changes.

Choose the strip first

Most 32650 and 32140 packs use 0.15 to 0.30 mm strip. Thicker strip carries more current but needs more weld energy. Start with plated 0.20 mm or pure 0.15 mm.

Method

  1. Clean the strip and cell terminal; no oil or dust.
  2. Use scrap cells and the same strip for test welds.
  3. Start at low energy and raise it in small steps until the weld holds.
  4. Pull-test each setting: the strip should tear, not peel off clean.
  5. Weld in a pattern that spreads heat, with two spots per terminal at least.

Heat control

Long weld pulses heat the cell. Avoid welding twice on the same spot and let the cell cool between welds. Wear gloves and keep a fire extinguisher nearby.

When your welder is not enough

Thick strip and large cells may need a stronger welder than a hobby one. If welds are weak, drop to thinner strip, add more weld spots, or change to bolted busbar designs. More help is in the weak weld troubleshooting guide.

Parts

Cells: Mocean 32140. Build example: the 4S4P pack.

Frequently asked questions

How many weld spots per terminal?

Two or more is normal. Use more for higher-current joints.

Why does my strip peel off?

Usually too little energy, dirty surfaces or poor electrode contact. Re-run test welds.

Can I solder instead?

Do not solder directly on cells. Heat from soldering can damage them.
