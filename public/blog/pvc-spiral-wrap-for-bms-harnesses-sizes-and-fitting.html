Insulation

PVC Spiral Wrap for BMS Wiring

Choose 3, 6, 9 or 12 mm and fit it

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

A BMS comes with a handful of thin balance leads. Spiral wrap turns them into one tidy cable, and it can be added after the wires are connected.

Why use spiral wrap

  • It bundles loose wires so they cannot snag or hang across live parts.
  • It protects the leads from abrasion against edges and each other.
  • It wraps on from the side, so you do not need to disconnect anything, and you can unwrap it to add a wire.

Choosing the size

SizeGood for
3 mmOne or two thin wires, such as a temperature sensor lead
6 mmA small bundle of balance leads, or a short harness
9 mmA BMS lead bundle for a larger series count
12 mmA thicker bundle, or a harness with a few power wires

To choose, measure the diameter of the bundle you want to cover, then choose the smallest size that winds on without forcing. A wrap that is too big leaves the bundle loose. One that is too small will not close around it.

Fitting it

  1. Finish and test the wiring first, and tidy the bundle.
  2. Start the spiral at one end and wind it along the bundle, turning it as you go.
  3. Leave the ends of the wires free, and check that each wire can still be reached.
  4. Secure the ends with a cable tie or a turn of tape so the spiral cannot unwind.

Where not to use it

  • For insulating a bare joint. Use heat shrink tube or tape.
  • In a spot that gets very hot. Keep it away from the welds and strips.
  • As the only protection for a high-current cable. That needs proper insulation of its own.

Sold per metre. Use it with a cable gland where the harness leaves the box, and see how to wire a BMS.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a wire later?

Yes. Unwind the spiral, add the wire and wind it back on.

Does spiral wrap insulate a joint?

No. It tidies and protects the bundle. Use heat shrink or tape for a bare joint.

How do I stop it unwinding?

Secure each end with a cable tie or a turn of tape.
