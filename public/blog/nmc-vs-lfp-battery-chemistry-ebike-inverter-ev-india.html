NMC vs LFP: Which Chemistry?

Energy, life, safety and cost compared

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

The two chemistries behind most lithium packs trade energy for life and cost. Match the chemistry to the job.

Side by side

NMCLFP
Nominal cell voltage3.6 V3.2 V
Energy per weightHigherLower
Cycle lifeGoodTypically longer
Thermal stabilityMore sensitiveMore tolerant
Best forLight, compact e-bikes and scootersInverters, storage, heavier vehicles

Guide by use

  • E-bike and light scooter: NMC, such as 21700 cells, for the best range per kilo.
  • Home inverter and solar storage: LFP, for life and heat tolerance; see 314Ah cells.
  • E-rickshaw and cargo: LFP for daily deep cycling.
  • Small gadgets: NMC 18650.

Different BMS, different charger

The chemistries have different voltage limits. Always use a BMS and charger made for your chemistry. See the series count table and our LFP solar guide.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Which is safer, NMC or LFP?

LFP is generally more thermally stable, but both are safe when well built with the right BMS and charger.

Which is lighter for the same energy?

NMC, because its cells store more energy per kilo.

Can I swap chemistries in a pack?

Not without changing the BMS and charger. Their voltage ranges differ.
