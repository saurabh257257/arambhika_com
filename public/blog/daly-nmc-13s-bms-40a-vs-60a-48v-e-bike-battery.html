Daly NMC 13S BMS: 40A vs 60A

Choosing a BMS for a 48V e-bike pack

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

13S is the standard series count for a 48 V NMC e-bike pack. The only real choice is the amp rating and whether you want Bluetooth.

40A or 60A?

At 46.8 V nominal, 40 A is about 1.9 kW and 60 A about 2.8 kW. A typical 500 W to 1000 W hub motor is covered by 40 A. Choose 60 A for stronger motors, or if you want headroom for uphill loads.

Models

  • 13S 40A smart and 13S 60A smart, with Bluetooth monitoring
  • 13S 40A hardware, the simplest and lowest-cost choice

Match it to the cells

A 13S4P pack of 4500 mAh cells such as the Norda 21700 must supply your peak current; confirm the cell discharge rating times four covers it. Our 13S build guide shows the full layout.

Charging

Use a charger made for 13S NMC, with a full-charge voltage near 54.6 V. A BMS is a safety net, not a replacement for the right charger.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Is 13S the same as 48V?

A 13S NMC pack is 46.8 V nominal and is sold as 48 V.

Do I need the smart version for an e-bike?

No, but it helps when you want to check cell balance or diagnose range loss.

Can one BMS handle a motor and a charger?

Most single-port BMS handle both through the same terminals, within the rating. Check the manual for your model.
