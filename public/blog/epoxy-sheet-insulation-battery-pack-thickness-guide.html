Epoxy Sheet for Battery Packs

Insulation thickness and where to use it

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Epoxy glass sheet is a stiff, flat insulator that can be cut to shape. Packs use it to keep metal parts apart and to protect cells from sharp edges. The right thickness depends on where it sits.

Where it is used

  • Between the busbar or strip and the pack case, so nothing can short to the metal.
  • Under and over the cell block as a base and cover plate.
  • Between cell groups where an extra barrier is needed.
  • As a mounting plate for the BMS or fuse.

Choosing the thickness

ThicknessTypical useProduct
0.5 mmThin barrier, wrap, tight spacesEpoxy sheet 502x338x0.5 mm
0.8 mmLight separation between partsEpoxy sheet 502x338x0.8 mm
1 mmGeneral base or cover plateEpoxy sheet 502x338x1 mm
2 mmStiffer mounting plateEpoxy sheet 502x338x2 mm

For large flat pieces there is also a longer 512x1225x0.5 mm sheet.

Cutting and handling

Cut with a fine blade or saw and wear a dust mask, since fibre dust is an irritant. Deburr the edges so they do not nick wires or cells. Check your pack design and the voltage it carries to confirm the thickness gives enough insulation.

Pair it with the rest of the pack

Epoxy sheet works alongside cell holders and potting compound to make a clean, safe assembly.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

Is epoxy sheet sold by weight?

Yes, our epoxy sheets are priced per kg. The listing shows the sheet size so you can work out how many sheets a kg covers.

Which thickness should I start with?

Many builders keep 0.5 mm for wrapping and 1 mm for base plates. Your design and voltage decide the final choice.

Can I drill epoxy sheet?

Yes, with sharp bits and support underneath to avoid chipping. Use a dust mask.
