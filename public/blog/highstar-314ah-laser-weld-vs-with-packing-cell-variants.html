Highstar 314Ah Cell Variants

Three listings, what each one is

By Arambhika · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Three Highstar 314Ah listings can look alike. The differences affect how you connect and transport the cells.

The listings

  • Highstar 314Ah 3.2V prismatic cell
  • Highstar LFP cell 314Ah with packing
  • Highstar LFP cell 314Ah laser welding

What the words mean

Laser welding refers to terminals prepared for laser-welded busbars. Pack builders with a laser welder use this for low-resistance, compact joints. The other types use bolted terminals. With packing means the cells ship in protective packaging suited to transport and storage. Confirm the exact terminal type and what is included on the product page.
