Epoxy Sheet Thickness Guide

Which of 0.5, 0.8, 1 or 2 mm to choose

By Arambhika · 23 November 2026 · 5 min read

The same sheet size comes in four thicknesses. A thicker sheet is stiffer and heavier, and a thinner one is easier to cut and fit.

About the material

FR-4 is fibreglass cloth soaked in epoxy resin and pressed under heat. Our sheets are 502 x 338 mm and, per the product data, are flame retardant to UL 94 V-0, with high dielectric strength and a glass transition temperature of 127 °C. They are sold per kg, with a 5 kg minimum.

Approximate weight per sheet

FR-4 has a density of about 1.85 g/cm3. A 502 x 338 mm sheet has an area of 1697 cm2, so:

ThicknessWeight per sheet, aboutSheets in 5 kg, aboutTypical use
0.5 mm157 g32Thin insulating barriers and liners
0.8 mm251 g20Barriers that need a little stiffness
1 mm314 g16Insulating plates, spacers, BMS mounting
2 mm628 g8Rigid end plates and structural spacers

These are estimates from density, so actual weights vary a little.

How to choose

  • Insulating between a busbar and a metal case. Go for 0.5 or 0.8 mm. Thin sheet is enough, and it follows the shape better.
  • Mounting a BMS or a plate that gets screwed down. Use 1 mm or more, so it does not flex.
  • Pack end plates or spacers that take load. Use 2 mm.
  • Unsure. Order a small quantity of two thicknesses and test the fit in your enclosure.

Sheets in our range: 0.5 mm, 0.8 mm, 1 mm and 2 mm. See also our post on epoxy sheet in battery packs and how to cut it safely.

General guidance only. Check cell, BMS and strip datasheets and the standards that apply to your product before you build or sell a pack.

Frequently asked questions

What does UL 94 V-0 mean?

It is a flammability rating. V-0 is the best of the vertical burn classes, meaning the material stops burning quickly.

How many sheets are in the minimum order?

At 5 kg, about 16 sheets of 1 mm, but the number depends on thickness. See the table.

Can I cut the sheet to size?

Yes, with the right tools and dust control. See our cutting guide.
